Anki Bot

Paste a YouTube lecture and get an Anki deck of flashcards on its key ideas. Each card links back to the moment in the video it came from.

The cards are written by Claude, using your key. Get one at console.anthropic.com. Your key is used for this one deck and never saved on our side.
