Anki Bot

Paste a YouTube lecture and get flashcards on its key ideas. Review and edit them, add your own, then download an Anki deck. Each card links back to the moment in the video it came from.

The cards are written by Claude, using your key. Get one at console.anthropic.com. Your key never reaches our server: your browser sends it straight to Anthropic, and it's forgotten when you close this tab.

Video won't load? Paste the transcript instead

Edit any card, delete the ones you don't want, or add your own. "Suggest a better version" asks Claude to rewrite a card to follow good flashcard rules.

The pasted transcript had no timestamps, so the cards can't link to the right moment in the video. Paste it with its timestamps for working links, or type a time on any card.
